Descriptive statistics, not financial advice. Volatility is the population standard deviation of percentage returns — scale-free, so coins at very different price levels compare directly. Outliers are observations beyond |Z| > 2 against each coin's own mean and σ; near-constant assets are flagged because a 0.03% move on a stablecoin registers as a 10σ event while meaning nothing economically.
